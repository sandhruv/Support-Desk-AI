Account Access - Support Desk Knowledge Base

Account recovery and access guidance.

1. Password reset
Customers can reset their password from the login page using "Forgot
password". A reset link is sent to the registered email address and expires
after 30 minutes. Support must never ask the customer to share their password
or one-time passcode.

2. Locked accounts
Accounts lock automatically after five failed login attempts for 15 minutes.
If the lock persists, a human support agent must verify the customer's identity
using the registered email and account history before unlocking.

3. Email change requests
Changing the registered email requires identity verification. Escalate to human
support. Agents should never reveal whether an email address exists on the
platform to an unverified requester.

4. Two-factor authentication (2FA)
If a customer loses access to their 2FA device, they must use a recovery code.
If no recovery code is available, escalate for manual identity verification.

5. Account deletion
Account deletion is permanent and requires a written confirmation from the
registered email address. Escalate to human support for processing.

Security rules:
- Never store plaintext passwords.
- Never ask customers to share passwords.
- Use the registered email on record for all recovery steps.
