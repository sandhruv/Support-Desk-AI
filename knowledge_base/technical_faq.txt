Technical FAQ - Support Desk Knowledge Base

Frequently asked technical questions and approved answers.

Q1. The application crashes whenever I try to log in. What should I do?
A. Update the application to the latest version, clear the app cache, restart
the device, then try again. If the crash persists, note the error code and
contact support.

Q2. The page keeps loading forever after signing in.
A. Force close the application, reopen it, and switch networks (Wi-Fi to
mobile data). If the problem continues, report the device model and app
version to support.

Q3. I receive a "500 Internal Server Error" when saving data.
A. Retry once after a minute. If the error persists, capture the timestamp and
the action you were performing and escalate to the technical team.

Q4. The application is very slow on my device.
A. Ensure at least 1 GB of free storage, close background applications, and
disable battery saver mode. Performance improves on devices with 4 GB RAM or
more.

Q5. Push notifications are not arriving.
A. Check notification permissions in system settings, disable and re-enable
notifications in the app, then restart the device.

Q6. The application does not install or update.
A. Check free storage, stable internet connection, and device compatibility
(Android 10+ or iOS 15+). Uninstall the old version only after confirming the
account email and password are available.
