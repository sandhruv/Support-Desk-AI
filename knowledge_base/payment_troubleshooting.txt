Payment Troubleshooting - Support Desk Knowledge Base

If a payment fails or shows an unexpected charge, follow these steps.

Step 1 - Verify the transaction
Ask for the transaction reference (TXNxxxx) and check the payment record.
Compare the amount, currency and status with what the customer reports.

Step 2 - Declined payments
A failed payment usually means the bank declined the charge. Ask the customer
to verify card details, expiry date and available balance, then retry. Declined
transactions do not deduct money; the bank releases any temporary authorization
hold within 3-5 business days.

Step 3 - Duplicate charges
If two completed charges exist for the same purchase, mark the case as a
duplicate charge and follow the refund policy. Do not promise an instant refund.

Step 4 - Missing records
If no payment record exists for the provided reference, do not guess. Escalate
the ticket to human support so the finance team can search the payment
provider records.

Step 5 - Communication
Explain clearly what was checked, what the policy allows, and the next step.
Never request full card numbers, CVV codes or passwords.
