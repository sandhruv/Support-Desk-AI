Refund Policy - Support Desk Knowledge Base

1. Scope
This policy covers duplicate charges, failed payments and refund requests for
subscription and one-time purchases.

2. Duplicate charges
If a customer was charged twice for the same purchase, support must collect the
transaction reference (for example TXN1001). The billing team reviews duplicate
charge reports within 5-7 business days. If the duplicate is confirmed, the
extra charge is refunded to the original payment method. Support agents must
never confirm an instant refund; they may confirm that the case is being
investigated.

3. Refund eligibility
- The transaction must be in "completed" status.
- The request must be made within 30 days of the charge.
- Pending or failed transactions are not eligible until they settle.
- Approved refunds appear on the original payment method within 5-7 business
  days after approval. Refund approval is performed by the finance team.

4. Invoice questions
Invoices are generated at the start of each billing cycle and can be resent to
the registered email address. Billing details can be updated in account
settings.

5. Escalation
Escalate to human support when the transaction reference is missing, when the
payment record cannot be found, when a chargeback is mentioned, or when the
customer disputes a refund decision.
